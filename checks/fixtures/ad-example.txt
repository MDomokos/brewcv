Mechanical Design Engineer, Single-Use Medical Devices

Northgate Dosing Systems is a fictional company. This advertisement is invented.

Join our team in Lisbon and own the mechanical design of a new drug-delivery device, from first sketch to tooling release.

What you will do
- Lead the mechanical design of injection-moulded drug-delivery devices, working in SolidWorks.
- Run design verification: build test rigs, write protocols and report the results.
- Qualify moulding suppliers and run first-article inspection with them.
- Hold design-for-manufacture reviews with toolmakers and close the issues before tooling release.
- Build tolerance stacks for small mechanisms and find interference early.
- Write design history file records under ISO 13485 and IEC 62366.
- Coach technicians on statistical process control for the moulding line.

What we are looking for
- A degree in mechanical engineering and experience in regulated product development.
- Strong GD&T and tolerance analysis.
- Hands-on prototyping: 3D printing, CNC milling and silicone tooling.
- Experience with Kanban boards and Agile ceremonies in a hardware team.
- Aerospace certification experience is a plus.
- Fluent English; Portuguese is welcome.
